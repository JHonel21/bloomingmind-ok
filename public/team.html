Clinical Team

Ashley Woltjer MS, LPC-S, NCC

Owner & Clinical Supervisor

My name is Ashley, and I am a mental health therapist and the owner of Blooming Mind PLLC in Jenks, Oklahoma.

As a Licensed Professional Counselor Supervisor (LPC-S) and National Certified Counselor (NCC), I work primarily with children and adolescents who struggle with behavioral, emotional, and communication difficulties related to autism, ADHD, sensory processing difficulties, and trauma responses.

I use an individualized approach with each client based on their specific needs and goals, but I naturally lean into a humanistic theoretical framework focusing on congruence, empathy, and unconditional positive regard throughout each session. I offer traditional talk therapy as well as a specialized variety of therapeutic approaches including interactive play therapy, art therapy, and infant mental health modalities, and animal-assisted therapy activities.

I believe that empowerment and comfortability drive people to create growth and change, so I strive to provide a safe environment for my clients to feel comfortable exploring their thoughts, expressing their feelings, and shaping their own values and worldviews. I assist in fostering diversity, advocacy, and inclusion with unconditional respect to all.

I obtained a Bachelor of Arts in psychology from Northeastern State University in 2016, a Master of Science in Counseling with an emphasis on clinical mental health from Northeastern State University in 2019, and a Master of Philosophy in Psychology from Walden University in 2024. I am currently a doctoral candidate at Walden University and am working toward completing a PhD in Psychology degree.

Insurance: Aetna, BCBS, Cigna/Evernorth, First Health, Health Choice, Healthcare Highways/90 Degree Benefits, Medica, United Healthcare (UHC), and United Medical Resources (UMR). Ashley also accepts private pay.

JF

Julie Foster MS, LPC-S

Clinical Supervisor

Hi, I'm Julie. I believe that life's "middle chapters" can often be the most challenging to navigate. Whether you are facing the complexities of divorce, the physical and emotional shifts of perimenopause, the transition to an empty nest, or the heavy weight of career burnout and aging parents, you don't have to carry it alone.

My path to counseling was built on a foundation of real-world experience. Before becoming a therapist, I spent 15 years as a Licensed Assisted Living Administrator. This gives me a unique, boots-on-the-ground understanding of the emotional and logistical toll that comes with caring for elderly loved ones. I also have an extensive background in community mental health, working with trauma and severe mental illness. My approach is warm, practical, and deeply rooted in the belief that your past experiences, no matter how messy, are your greatest strengths.

As a lifelong resident of Broken Arrow, I raised my son here as a single parent and understand the grit it takes to build a resilient, meaningful life. I bring that same authenticity into our sessions, providing a safe, non-judgmental space where we can untangle the "what now?" of your life together.

When I'm not in the office, I'm likely cheering on my favorite college football team, experimenting in the kitchen, or planning my next travel adventure. I am also desperately trying to improve my golf game, though my two dogs, Jack and Goose, seem much more impressed by my cooking than my swing.

I earned my Bachelor of Science in Psychology and went on to successfully complete my Master of Science in Clinical Mental Health Counseling.

Insurance: Most major insurance plans. Julie also accepts private pay.

SK

Shelly Kerr LPC-C

LPC Candidate

My name is Shelly and I am currently a licensed professional counselor candidate. My path to counseling began in the classroom. Fourteen years as a teacher taught me patience, empathy, and the art of listening between the lines. Those skills only deepened when I earned my master's degree in Mental Health Counseling from the University of Oklahoma in 2024 and stepped into my role as a therapist.

My background in science, education, and counseling has shaped me into a lifelong learner and dedicated helper. I connect easily, communicate clearly, and find joy in helping others discover tools for emotional balance. I use a variety of approaches because I realize everyone has unique issues and may require a different type of therapy. Two of my favorite types of therapy are acceptance commitment therapy (ACT), which focuses on being present, discovering values, and taking actions according to those values, and Gestalt therapy, which helps clients make meaning out of their experiences.

Insurance: Oklahoma Medicaid (Soonercare/SoonerSelect) plans. Shelly also accepts private pay.
