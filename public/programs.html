Assessments & Classes

Beyond weekly therapy, Blooming Mind offers focused programs for specific needs. Our featured program is a one-day therapy intensive for men. Individual assessments and group classes will be listed below as they open.

The Secure Male · Attachment Recovery Intensive

Break the Pattern. Keep the Connection.

A one-day, 8-hour therapy intensive for men who shut down, blow up, or pull away from the people they love.

  • 8 hrs focused 1:1 therapy in a single day
  • 2 weeks of guided support: 1 week of prep before, 1 week of practice after
  • 4 research-backed approaches: EFT, IFS, DBT, CBT
Request a Consult

Is this you?

  • You go quiet or leave the room when conflict heats up
  • Anger shows up before you can think it through
  • You worry you're not enough, or need constant reassurance
  • Relationships stall or end just as they get serious

Why an intensive?

A full, uninterrupted day gives you about as much therapy time as two months of weekly sessions, with room to go deeper and practice new skills on the spot. Progress is measured before and after.

Your facilitator

Julie Foster, MS, LPC-S leads this program at Blooming Mind in Jenks. Sessions are available in person or by telehealth. Read Julie's bio.

What's included

  1. Intake & attachment assessment
  2. Client workbook + 7 days of guided prep
  3. 8-hour private intensive
  4. 7 days of guided practice + check-in call
  5. Follow-up session to review your results
